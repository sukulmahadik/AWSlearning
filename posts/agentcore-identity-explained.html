Every outbound call starts from a verified login and ends with a credential scoped to one agent and one user.

AI agents call many services on behalf of many people, and every one of those calls needs a credential. AgentCore Identity is AWS's answer: it checks who calls the agent, gives the agent an identity of its own, and keeps each user's tokens in a vault so the agent's code never holds a secret. This guide builds up the ideas one at a time, and pairs with Cognito, Explained.
